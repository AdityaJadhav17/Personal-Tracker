# Code of conduct

Personal Tracker is one person's tool, made in public. Anyone who opens an
issue, comments or sends a change is welcome, and is expected to:

- Be kind and assume good faith. Criticise code and ideas, never people.
- Keep it on topic: the app, its code, its docs.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone else's personal information, and never
  ask anyone for theirs. That includes exports, screenshots of real data, and
  anything from a locked note.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexual
content, threats, and publishing anyone's private information.

The maintainer, Aditya Jadhav, will remove content and block accounts that
break this. Report a problem privately through the
[security advisory form](https://github.com/AdityaJadhav17/Personal-Tracker/security/advisories/new)
or by messaging @AdityaJadhav17 on GitHub.

This follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
