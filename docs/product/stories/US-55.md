# US-55, dates first

Reported 24 September 2026 with a screenshot: Later listed the December finals
above the Oct 7 homework, and with US-54 showing ten, October work fell behind
"Show 9 more". Aditya: "show the upcoming with correct hierarchy of dates".
Built the same day.

```
US-55  As someone reading Home to decide what is next,
       I want every group in date order,
       so that the top of the list is what comes first.

Priority: Must, built
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-55.1  Inside a group, a sooner day comes first, whatever its priority.
  AC-55.2  On the same day, high comes before normal before low, even when
           the high one is due later that day.
  AC-55.3  In Overdue, the most overdue comes first, whatever its priority.
  AC-55.4  The ten Home shows are the ten soonest, so a high item due in
           December never takes a slot from one due next week.
```

Supersedes AC-04.1 for items on different days. AC-04.1's own test, three
items due the same day, still holds, as do AC-04.2 to AC-04.4 and AC-03.3.

## Decisions

**Every group, not only Later.** Aditya's pick of two. The other kept Today
and This week priority first. One rule everywhere is easier to read, and a
high item keeps its accent bar, so an exam still stands out without jumping
the queue.

**Priority still orders one day.** A midterm at 3pm reads above a reading due
at 9am the same day: on the day itself, what matters most is the question.

**Why it surfaced now.** Priority first was harmless while Home showed
everything. US-54 cut the list at ten in display order, and the display order
let December in ahead of October.
