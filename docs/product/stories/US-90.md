# US-90, US holidays on the calendar

Asked for on 1 October 2026. Federal holidays and the common days that are
not days off, picked over federal only.

```
US-90  As someone planning a term around days off,
       I want US holidays on the calendar,
       so that I see Thanksgiving and Memorial Day without looking them up.

Priority: Should
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-90.1  The calendar shows each holiday as a quiet label in its day's
           cell and in the open day, in any year.
  AC-90.2  Federal holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day,
           Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day,
           Labor Day, Indigenous Peoples' Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving
           and Christmas Day.
  AC-90.3  When a fixed-date federal holiday falls on a Saturday it is also
           marked "(observed)" on the Friday before; on a Sunday, on the
           Monday after.
  AC-90.4  Common days, not days off: Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Father's
           Day, Halloween, Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve.
  AC-90.5  A holiday is not a deadline: no done circle, nothing on Home,
           not counted in a week's load, never exported.
```

## Decisions

**Worked out, not downloaded.** Every date is a rule: a fixed day, or "the
fourth Thursday of November", "the last Monday of May". A pure function in
`src/domain/` computes a year's list, so there is no network request, no file
to keep current, and it is right in 2031. Importing a holiday `.ics` would
have made each one a deadline to tick off.

**Indigenous Peoples' Day.** The federal name is Columbus Day; California and
UC San Diego mark the second Monday of October as Indigenous Peoples' Day,
and that is the name shown.

**Not in this story.** Easter and other moveable religious holidays, UCSD's
own academic calendar, and a switch to hide holidays.
