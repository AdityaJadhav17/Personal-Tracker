# US-66, the phone's floating tab bar

Picked from three prototypes on 24 September 2026: Floating, over Overflow
and Merged.

```
US-66  As someone who checks deadlines on my phone,
       I want labelled tabs at the bottom where my thumb is, and adding one
       tap away from any view,
       so that the app feels like an iPhone app and not a shrunk website.

Priority: Should
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-66.1  Up to 700px wide, a floating capsule at the foot of the screen
           holds four labelled tabs: Home, Calendar, Goals and More. The
           sidebar's title and the icon row are gone.
  AC-66.2  More opens a sheet listing Courses, Reflections, Trends, Data and
           the theme button. Choosing one goes there and closes the sheet.
           More is marked current while you are on any of those four, and
           carries the backup dot.
  AC-66.3  A round + beside the capsule opens a sheet with the add form, on
           every view. Adding closes it; the new item is on Home. Home's
           inline field is hidden on a phone, except while Home is empty,
           so "Add your first item" still has a field to go to.
  AC-66.4  Sheets rise from the bottom and leave the same way. Escape, a tap
           outside, or choosing something closes them. Reduced motion fades
           instead.
  AC-66.5  Nothing hides behind the capsule: the page, the undo message and
           the last row all clear it, and the home indicator's inset is
           respected.
  AC-66.6  No field is smaller than 16px on a phone, so iOS does not zoom
           into it.
  AC-66.7  Wider than 700px, nothing changes: the sidebar as it is.
```
