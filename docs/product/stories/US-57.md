# US-57, Home as a timeline

From the UI audit of 24 September 2026, asked for as "modern, not AI slop".
Aditya picked the Timeline direction from a three-way prototype (Quiet,
Timeline, Dense) and accepted all three recommended defaults. Built the same
day.

```
US-57  As someone who opens Home many times a day,
       I want it to read like an agenda rather than a dashboard,
       so that what is next is the first thing I see and nothing is noise.

Priority: Should, built
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-57.1  Home opens on today's date as its heading, and one line that says
           how many are due today, overdue and this week, and how many were
           finished yesterday. Today is always said, "Nothing due today" at
           zero; the rest appear only when there is something to count.
  AC-57.2  The add form is one line, "Add a deadline…", until used. Using it
           opens Due (starting on today), Time, Category, Priority and Repeat
           as a panel over the list; adding, leaving it or Escape folds it.
  AC-57.3  Overdue items keep their own group, each saying how late it is.
  AC-57.4  Upcoming items sit under their day: the date's number with
           "Today", "Tomorrow" or the weekday under it, the month named where
           it changes. Each day is a heading named by its full date.
  AC-57.5  A row shows its time only when it is not 11:59pm, and no category
           tag or Soon marker.
  AC-57.6  Under the title, in one quiet line: course, goal, repeat, steps
           and the note.
  AC-57.7  High priority reads bold.
  AC-57.8  Each course has one colour, by the order courses were added, and
           every colour reaches 3:1 against the page and a card.
```

Supersedes US-02's Today, This week and Later headings; US-12's Soon marker,
whose `isUpcoming` went with it; US-16's two big numbers, now words; US-44's
hint sentence; AC-54.5's group counts; and the full date on each row. The
facts those stories required are still tested, under their old IDs where the
meaning held.

## Decisions

**Timeline, over Quiet and Dense.** All three shared the header, the one-line
add, relative dates and course colours; they differed in the list. The
timeline answers "what is on Sunday" by reading down a column.

**Notes stay, as a second line.** The prototype hid them. Syllabus notes carry
real information, such as a testing window, so they show, cut to one line.

**The date starts on today, and Soon is gone.** The day column already says
how close something is.

**The panel floats; it does not push.** The first build opened the fields in
the page's flow and folded them on blur, so the list jumped up by their height
mid-click and the click landed on the wrong row. Playwright found it across
dozens of specs. The fields now open as a panel over the list, and fold after
an add so they never cover the row just added.

**Course colours are derived, not stored.** Blue, amber, violet, pink, by the
order in `courses`. Nothing new in the database, so no migration.

**Two level-one headings.** The sidebar's name and the date. HTML allows it,
and the date is what this view is about.

## What it took

Day entries are list items, so every test that counted list items to count
items now counts done controls instead; `e2e/helpers.ts` gained `doneControls`,
`row` and `dayName`. The contrast test's token pattern did not allow digits,
so it could not see `--course-1` until it did.

The content column is centred on every view, from the same audit.
