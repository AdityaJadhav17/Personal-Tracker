# US-93, change an event's category

Asked for on 4 October 2026, after US-92 added priority to the open event and
noted that category was the one thing set while adding that still could not be
changed.

```
US-93  As someone who filed a work meeting as Academic by mistake,
       I want to change its category where I change everything else,
       so that the filters show it where it belongs.

Priority: Should
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-93.1  An open event, on Home or in the calendar, has a Category select
           showing its category. Picking another saves at once.
  AC-93.2  (replaces AC-92.3) Category and Priority share a row below Due,
           Time and Repeat; Course and Goal share the next.
```

## Decisions

**Two rows of two.** Four selects do not fit a row of three, and halves keep
each pair readable: what kind of thing it is and how much it matters, then what
it belongs to.
