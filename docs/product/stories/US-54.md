# US-54, ten at a time

Asked for 24 September 2026, after the syllabus import filled Home with the
whole term: "the list feels overwhelming at times". Built the same day.

```
US-54  As someone with a term's deadlines in the list,
       I want Home to show the next ten rather than all of them,
       so that I can see what matters without scrolling past November.

Priority: Should, built
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-54.1  Given more than ten upcoming items, Home shows the first ten in the
           order it already sorts them, and says how many of how many.
  AC-54.2  Every overdue item is shown, and none of them counts towards the
           ten.
  AC-54.3  Show N more reveals the rest and Show fewer goes back to ten. A
           reload shows ten again.
  AC-54.4  With ten or fewer upcoming, everything shows and nothing is said.
  AC-54.5  A group cut short still counts everything in it.
```

## Decisions

**Overdue stays whole.** Aditya's call. A missed deadline hidden behind "more"
is the pile US-44 exists to empty.

**Filters apply first.** Home is handed the filtered list, so choosing
Academic gives the next ten academic items rather than the academic ones that
happened to be in the first ten.

**The cut follows the display order,** Today, then This week, then Later, and
high priority first inside each. So the ten are the ten you would have read
first anyway.

**Counts stay whole.** "Later 11" with four rows showing is still true, and
together with "Showing 10 of 14 upcoming" it says where the rest are.

**Not remembered,** like the filters and for the same reason: a list that
silently shows everything after a reload, or silently hides things, is lying
to you about which one it is.

**The calendar is unchanged.** It is where the whole term belongs.
