# US-67, finishing and removing feel final, and forgivable

From the UI list of 25 September 2026, items 1 and 2.

```
US-67  As someone who ticks things off many times a day,
       I want finishing to look like finishing, and removing to act at once
       with a way back,
       so that the most frequent action feels good and cleanup costs one
       click instead of two.

Priority: Should
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-67.1  Ticking an item fills its circle with a check at once; the row
           then folds away and the item leaves the list. The undo message
           appears as it goes. With reduced motion it leaves at once.
  AC-67.2  Ticking two items in quick succession finishes both.
  AC-67.3  Delete in an open row and Drop on an overdue row act at once,
           with no question. The message says "Deleted X." and Undo brings
           it back, steps included.
  AC-67.4  The undo message and u work on every view, so a delete from the
           calendar's day can be undone there.
  AC-67.5  Undo reaches back one step: finishing or deleting something else
           replaces what it would undo.
```

## Decisions

**Courses and goals still ask.** Deleting one clears it from every item that
pointed at it, and putting those links back is a different undo from putting
an item back. They are deleted a few times a term, so the question costs
little there.

**The save happens before the fold.** Ticking saves at once, and Home keeps
the row on screen for the 400ms it takes to fold, then lets it go. A first
version saved when the fold ended, and a reload inside those 400ms lost the
tick; the Playwright specs that reload straight after ticking caught it.
