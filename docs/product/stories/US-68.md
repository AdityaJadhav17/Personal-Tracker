# US-68, neighbouring dates in the calendar

From the UI list of 25 September 2026, item 4.

```
US-68  As someone reading a month at a glance,
       I want the grey days either side of it to say which dates they are,
       so that the grid reads as a run of real days, as a calendar app's does.

Priority: Could
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-68.1  Days before the 1st and after the last show their own date
           number, dimmed: September 2026 opens with 30 and 31 and closes
           with 1, 2 and 3.
  AC-68.2  They stay out of the way: no items, not clickable, and hidden
           from a screen reader, which already hears each real day in full.
```
