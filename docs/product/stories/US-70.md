# US-70, edits save as you go

From the final UI polish plan, 25 September 2026
([ui-final-polish-plan.md](../../design/ui-final-polish-plan.md)).

```
US-70  As someone fixing a date or a title,
       I want the change kept when I am done with it, as the note already is,
       so that no edit is lost to a Save I did not press.

Priority: Should
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-70.1  Title, date and time are saved when focus leaves the open row, or
           on Enter in one of them.
  AC-70.2  Repeat, course and goal are saved the moment one is picked.
  AC-70.3  A blank title or a missing date shows its message under the
           field and saves nothing; what you typed stays for you to fix.
  AC-70.4  There is no Save button. Delete stays, at the far end.
  AC-70.5  Leaving a row you did not change saves nothing.
```

## Decisions

**The row, not the field.** Saving the date the moment you leave the date
field would move the item to its new day before you reach the time, and the
row would close under you. The row is the unit you edit, so leaving it is
the moment to save.
