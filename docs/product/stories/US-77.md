# US-77, edit a course

Asked for on 27 September 2026, with a CSE 158R card whose professor and
office hours were still blank.

```
US-77  As someone who learns a course's details a week into the term,
       I want to change a course after adding it,
       so that filling in the rest does not mean deleting it and re-linking
       everything.

Priority: Should
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-77.1  A course's More offers Edit beside Delete. Edit turns the card
           into the course form, holding what the course says now, with
           Save course and Cancel.
  AC-77.2  Saving keeps the course itself: its items stay linked and its
           colour does not change. Cancel changes nothing.
  AC-77.3  A course still needs a name: a blank one says so and saves
           nothing.
```

## Decisions

**In the card, not at the top.** The form opens where the course is, so it
is plain which one you are changing.
