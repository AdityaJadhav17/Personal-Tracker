# US-89, a deadline typed into a closing day is kept

Reported on 1 October 2026: adding an event from the calendar and clicking
out of the day lost it; only pressing Add kept it.

```
US-89  As someone adding things to the calendar,
       I want what I typed to be kept when I click away,
       so that I do not lose a deadline because I did not press Add.

Priority: Must
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-89.1  A calendar day closed with a title typed in its form adds that
           deadline, on that day, however the day closes: a click outside,
           another day chosen, the month turned, or Close.
  AC-89.2  A day closed with an empty title adds nothing.
  AC-89.3  The phone's + sheet does the same when it closes with a title
           typed.
```

## Decisions

**Why it happened.** The day is a native popover. A click outside closes it,
which unmounts the form and everything typed in it. Nothing was wrong with
Add; closing simply never asked.

**Kept, not asked about.** As Apple Calendar keeps a new event when you click
away, and as US-70 made edits save as you go. A deadline added by mistake is
one Delete, with Undo, away.

**Only where closing loses the form.** The calendar day and the phone sheet.
Home's add field keeps what is typed when you click away, so it is left as
it is.
