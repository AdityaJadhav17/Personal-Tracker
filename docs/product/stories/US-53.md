# US-53, add from the calendar

Asked for 24 September 2026: "if i click on the calendar i can add an event".
Built into the variant Aditya picked from the phase 5 prototype of the
[UI polish plan](../../design/ui-polish-plan.md). Built the same day.

```
US-53  As someone planning a term from the calendar,
       I want to click a day and add something to it there,
       so that I do not go back to Home and retype a date I was looking at.

Priority: Should, built
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-53.1  Pressing any part of a day, the empty part included, opens it
           beside its cell with the title field focused.
  AC-53.2  A title and Enter adds it on that day, due at 23:59.
  AC-53.3  Time, category, priority and repeat work as on Home.
  AC-53.4  The day is the date, so the form has no date field.
  AC-53.5  After adding, the title clears and keeps focus for the next one.
  AC-53.6  Turning the month closes the open day.
  AC-53.7  Escape closes the day and gives focus back to what opened it.
```

Supersedes where US-41 put the open day (below the grid) and the rise in
AC-48.2 for it, which is now a scale from its cell. US-41's criteria about
what the day contains still hold.

## Decisions

**Anchored, picked from three.** A throwaway page showed the day below the
grid, in a pane beside it, and as a popover by the cell, each with the add
form, on data shaped like October. Aditya picked the popover. apple-design's
rule is that something opens where you reached for it; below the grid, on a
five-row month, the form landed under the fold.

**Native, not built.** The `popover` attribute gives the top layer, Escape and
closing on a click outside. CSS anchor positioning keeps it beside its cell
and flips it left or upwards near an edge. No positioning code and no
dependency. React 18 does not know the attribute, so the component sets it.

**The whole add form, not a short one.** The prototype had title, time and
category. The real one reuses `AddItemForm` with the date fixed, so priority
and repeat come along and there is one form to keep right, not two.

**Closes when the month turns.** US-41 kept the day open across months. A
popover pointing at a cell that is no longer on screen is worse than closing.
Light dismiss only hears the pointer, so the month buttons close it
themselves, which covers the keyboard.

**Focus.** Into the title on open, so click, type, Enter is the whole
gesture. Back to what opened it on Escape or Close, but not when the popover
closed because you clicked something else: that would take focus away from
what you just chose.

## What it took

jsdom has no popover API, so `test-setup.ts` stubs `showPopover` and
`hidePopover` and component tests see the day in the document. Escape, light
dismiss and placement are checked in Playwright, which asserts the popover
sits against its cell's side.
