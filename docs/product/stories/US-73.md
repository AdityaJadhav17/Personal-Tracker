# US-73, Home's header collapses as you scroll

Picked from three prototypes on 25 September 2026: Collapsing, over Pinned
and Sections. The idea came from the final UI polish plan
([ui-final-polish-plan.md](../../design/ui-final-polish-plan.md)).

```
US-73  As someone scrolling into October on Home,
       I want today's date and how it stands to stay in view,
       without the whole header eating the list,
       so that I always know where "now" is while reading ahead.

Priority: Could
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-73.1  As Home scrolls, the large date fades and shrinks away and a slim
           bar slides in at the top: the date, small, and the first two
           counts ("1 due today · 1 overdue"). Scrolling back reverses it.
  AC-73.2  The bar is translucent: the list blurs under it. With reduced
           transparency it is solid.
  AC-73.3  It follows the scroll exactly, with no timer, so it is as fast as
           the hand and can be reversed at any point.
  AC-73.4  A screen reader hears the date once, from the page heading; the
           bar repeats it only for the eye.
  AC-73.5  Only Home has it. A browser without scroll-driven animations
           shows Home as it was.
```
