# US-75, finished items stay on the calendar

Asked for on 27 September 2026: "mark them done and completed and they still
stay there on the calendar". Replaces AC-21.6, which took them off.

```
US-75  As someone looking back over a month,
       I want what I finished to stay on its day, marked done,
       so that the calendar is also a record of what I got through.

Priority: Should
Acceptance criteria:
  AC-75.1  A finished item stays on the day it was due, marked done: a
           check, muted, struck through, its course colour kept. Open items
           come first in a day, finished ones after.
  AC-75.2  A finished item cannot be dragged to another day.
  AC-75.3  The week's count and "N more" are unchanged by it: the count is
           open work ahead, and finished items never push an open one out of
           a cell's two visible places.
  AC-75.4  Opening the day lists finished items under the open ones, as a
           quiet "Done" list with nothing to edit.
  AC-75.5  Home and the phone calendar export are unchanged: finished items
           still leave Home, and the export is open deadlines only.
```

## Decisions

**No reopen, no toggle.** Undo covers a tick by mistake. Reopening something
days later, and hiding finished items on a busy month, are each their own
small story if they are wanted.
